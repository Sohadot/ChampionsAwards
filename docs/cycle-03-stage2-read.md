**4. Has a second mechanism appeared?** **No.** Three new cases, zero new

**5. Unexplained under-recognition.** **1 of 3** — Flowers, at a gap of 27. It was

**6. Ontology coverage.** 1 of 6, unchanged by three cases.

**7. Did Flowers reveal a new mechanism, or an evidence boundary?** **Neither an
evidence gap nor a new mechanism: a boundary in the ontology.** Every element of
his case is documented by the state agency that imposed the secrecy — Flowers as
the designer, everyone sworn to the Official Secrets Act, eight of ten machines
destroyed, the order to hand over all documentation, the existence revealed only
in the early 2000s. The evidence is not missing. What is missing is a concept:

- **Institutional exclusion** requires a formal barrier to an appointment,
  membership, authorship or pay. Nothing was closed to him; he held a senior
  engineering post throughout.
- **Delayed recognition** turns on a community unable or unready to absorb a
  contribution. This community was legally prevented from knowing it existed. The
  timing matches and the cause does not.
- **Institutional gatekeeping** is the closest fit and still fails: it describes a
  person holding editorial, institutional or reviewing authority obstructing
  dissemination. A wartime classification regime binding everyone under statute is
  a different object, and stretching the concept to cover it is the accommodation
  this project refuses elsewhere.

**No concept was created for it.** A mechanism invented to fit the single case
that motivated it is a taxonomy built backwards from a result — the error removed
from the Definition of Done in Cycle 01. If a second case of this shape appears,
the corpus will have grounds it does not have now.

**8. Did Zhang and Sammet show that lower DDI does not mean lower recognition?**
**Yes, and unambiguously.** The two lowest assessed cases in the domain are both
in the aligned band, and Sammet — the joint-lowest index — has the densest
anchored recognition record in the whole corpus: nine independent bodies between
1965 and 2013. The merit side and the recognition side of this instrument are not
locked together, which is the first time the corpus has been able to show it.

**9. Award interactions.** **4, unchanged.** Neither Zhang nor Sammet nor Flowers
carries a relation, and none was manufactured. Zhang was well past forty when his
result appeared and this project published a Fields Medal age-rule anatomy in the
same cycle, so the relation was available and tempting; no record shows the rule
was applied to him, that he was considered, or that any body commented on his
eligibility, so there is no relation. The Wiles entry earns its eligibility
relation because the Congress's own press office stated the rule at the time. An
inference from a birth date is not a documented interaction.
