## The question this anatomy was built to settle

> Does the cross-cutting system actually add a layer of comparison between the
> two halves of this domain, or is it merely a multi-branch award?

**Neither.** It is a third thing the corpus had no category for.

The Wolf Prize has nine fields — Agriculture, Chemistry, Mathematics, Medicine
and Physics in the sciences; Architecture, Music, Painting and Sculpture in the
arts — and **no computing field**. No Wolf jury can compare a mathematician with
a computer scientist, so this award cannot bridge the join. Within Mathematics &
Computing it operates on one side only, and the `subfield: cross-cutting` label
Round 1 gave it is corrected to `mathematics`.

What it is instead is the project's **first cross-domain apparatus**. It has
recognised two governed cases and they sit in two domains this project governs
separately:

| Case | Domain | Award | Year | Prize share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Andrew Wiles | mathematics-computing | Wolf Prize in Mathematics | 1995/6 | shared with Robert P. Langlands |
| Chien-Shiung Wu | physics-astronomy | Wolf Prize in Physics | 1978 | held alone |

Wu was not anticipated. She is a corpus case from Cycle 01 carrying a gap of 18,
and the foundation's record shows her holding the **inaugural** physics prize
outright. That relation existed in the world before this round and was invisible
to the corpus for the same reason the Turing relation was: no anatomy.

## An engine change the anatomy forced

Every anatomy before this one sat inside a single domain, so the award layer and
the domain layer were the same shape and an award could carry one `domain`. This
one cannot. An award anatomy may now declare `domains` in the plural, as
recognition systems always could — and, the part that matters, **a relation is
counted in the domain of the case it names, not of the award that carries it**.
Counting Wu's award outcome inside a mathematics corpus would have been a false
figure. Sector pages are filtered by the same rule, so a page and its own
denominator cannot disagree, and the sector-page invariant was corrected rather
than relaxed: it had compared one award's relations to the whole domain's
denominator, which only held while every domain had a single single-field
anatomy.

## Three systems, three answers to what a recognition system owes the public

With three anatomies in one domain the comparison is now element by element, and
the transparency picture has no ordering.

| | Fields Medal | Turing Award | Wolf Prize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nomination | open to anyone; committee may consider the un-nominated | not readable | by invitation only |
| Who judges | every committee published since 1936, with recusals | not readable | never published |
| Nominations | confidential, no release horizon | not readable | never published; valid 3 years |
| Outcomes | laureates and citations | laureates and citations | laureates, citations, affiliation at the time, prize share |
| Funding | university trust, called underfunded by the union | single commercial sponsor | family endowment; juries serve unpaid |
| Governing document | published Statutes | not readable | none published |

The Fields Medal is maximally open about **who decided** and permanently closed
about **who was considered**. The Wolf Prize is the reverse, and more detailed
than either on outcomes. The Turing Award publishes criteria and laureates and
nothing else this audit could read. No single transparency score expresses that,
which is why the instrument scores transparency as one dimension of five rather
than as a verdict.

## One claim flagged and not carried forward

The recognition-system entry for this prize states that in several fields it has
reliably anticipated later Nobel recognition. The foundation does not say so, and
establishing it would mean compiling its laureate list against another award's —
this project's own analysis presented as a documented fact. The anatomy records
the exception and asserts nothing. The claim is left live on the system entry and
is now a known open item for its requalification, not something this round
silently repaired.
