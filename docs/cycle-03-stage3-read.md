## The five readings

**1. Did delayed recognition actually appear?** **Yes.** Green is the first case in
this domain to evidence it and the corpus's second anywhere. The fit is by
definition rather than resemblance: the concept turns on time and reception rather
than contested credit, and names publication through a low-visibility channel
among its routes. The record supplies each clause — an essay sold by subscription
to fifty-one people, eighteen years of silence, a rediscovery by a third party
five years after the author's death, a reprint in a foreign journal thereafter,
and no rival claimant at any point. Three alternative mechanisms were tested and
refused: nothing was closed to him, nobody took his credit, and no eligibility
rule foreclosed an honour.

Support is 1 of 10, so it is **emergent, not established**. One case does not make
a pattern, and the threshold was not bent for the mechanism the round was designed
around — the same discipline applied to Just in Cycle 02.

**2. Did the Flowers boundary recur?** **Yes, and the tests failed identically.**
Ellis conceived public-key cryptography in a report classified Secret in January
1970; it stayed classified until December 1997, a month after he died; his own
papers were released in 2016. Exclusion fails — he was a GCHQ employee and nothing
was barred to him. Delayed recognition fails on cause rather than timing — this
community was demonstrably able to absorb the idea, since it produced the same
concept independently within about six years, but was legally prevented from
knowing his existed. Gatekeeping fails — a statutory classification regime is not
the person exercising editorial or institutional discretion the concept describes.

One test was run harder here than anywhere: **credit misattribution**, because the
field's highest award for public-key cryptography is held by other people. It is
still refused. Diffie, Hellman, Merkle, Rivest, Shamir and Adleman did not displace
him; they arrived independently by another route in ignorance of his work.
Displacement is what the definition requires, and independent rediscovery is a
different event — one that cuts the other way, since it is the reason his
uniqueness score is the most bounded in the corpus.

**No concept was created, and two occurrences are not being counted as a pattern.**
Both cases were produced by the same national cryptographic apparatus, so a shape
that recurs inside one secrecy regime may describe that regime rather than a
mechanism of recognition. Establishing it would need a case from a different
regime. That adjudication belongs to the ontology review, not to the entry that
motivated it.

Three things stand out, and the third is the one to carry into synthesis.

**This domain aligns where the others do not.** Half its cases sit in the band
where recognition roughly matches assessed merit; physics has none and biology
two. Its median gap is a third of theirs.

**Its merit range is now comparable and its recognition range is not.** The DDI
spread reached 11 against 13 and 14 — the compression that made the Stage 1 read
withdraw an inference is gone, and it closed because two genuinely narrower
contributions were entered, not because a target was set.

**It produces fewer mechanisms per deficit than either closed domain.** Physics
explains 9 of its 10 deficits; biology 5 of 8; this domain 3 of 5, and its two
unexplained cases both stand on the same boundary in the vocabulary rather than on
missing evidence. Every fact in both is documented by the state agency that
imposed the secrecy. **The corpus is not short of evidence here. It is short of a
concept — and it has deliberately not invented one.**

## What this round deliberately did not do

- It did not preassign delayed recognition to Green. The mechanism was tested
  against the definition after the research, and three alternatives were refused
  on the record.
- It did not create a secrecy mechanism for Ellis, and did not let a second
  occurrence promote an unnamed shape into an established pattern.
- It did not enter a case to populate the cross-cutting compartment.
- It did not manufacture an award relation. Award interactions stand at 4, the
  same figure as at n=5.
