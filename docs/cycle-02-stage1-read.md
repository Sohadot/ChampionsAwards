Two observations worth recording, both engine-derived and neither a conclusion:

- **The asymmetry replicates across domains.** In physics, assessed DDI spans 13
  points against 34 points of observed recognition. In biology it is now 14
  against 37. The same shape appeared in a corpus built at a different time from
  different cases — which is what would have to happen for the physics hypothesis
  about the recognition side to survive. It is not yet a test of that hypothesis;
  both corpora were purposively assembled, and a shared selection process would
  produce a shared shape.
- **No co-occurrence at all.** Not one biology case carries two evidenced
  mechanisms. In physics, every co-occurring pair included credit misattribution.
  Here the mechanisms do not stack.

## What the corpus still lacks — for stage 2

These are gaps in the dataset, stated so the last three cases are chosen against
evidence rather than against a wish list:

1. **No case where recognition exceeds assessed merit.** Every gap in both
   domains is positive or near zero. The instrument has never been shown
   producing a negative gap, which is its most obvious untested behaviour.
2. **One mechanism carries the domain.** Credit misattribution is the only
   established mechanism; institutional exclusion, which is established in
   physics at 4/10, has no biology case at all. Whether that is a real
   difference between fields or an artefact of these seven cases is currently
   undecidable from the corpus.
3. **Almost every case meets the same recognition system.** Six of seven
   interact with the Nobel apparatus. Only Ball's case is decided entirely
   outside it, and only Hilleman's turns on Lasker. A domain whose gaps are all
   measured against one institution is measuring that institution.
4. **Narrow geography.** All seven are European or North American. Physics has
   one non-Western case (Bose); biology has none.
5. **Unexplained is now the largest single group** — 3 of 7. If that persists,
   it becomes the domain's finding rather than a shortfall, and the synthesis
   should treat it as one.

No names are proposed here. The point of stopping at seven is that the next
three should answer one or more of the five gaps above, and that choice is the
author's, not the engine's.
