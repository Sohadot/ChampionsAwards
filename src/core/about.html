About

Why this exists

Recognition shapes what a civilization remembers. When it misfires, the record — not just a career — is distorted. This project studies that machinery in the open.

The problem

Awards and honors are treated as conclusions when they are really outputs of a process — one that can reward the visible over the significant, the timely over the durable, and the well-placed over the overlooked. The gap between who is recognized and who contributed is real, recurring, and rarely measured.

The approach

Rather than issuing opinions, {{ site.site_name }} publishes a method and applies it consistently: a weighted index for contributions, a legitimacy assessment for systems, and an archive for documented omissions — all bound by an editorial protocol of sourcing, neutrality, and correction.

What we are honest about

This is a young project with a small published record. Its scores are structured estimates, not measurements, and its weights are a defensible choice open to challenge. Its value will be earned slowly, one well-sourced entry at a time — not declared. That trade is deliberate.

Contact & contributions

Corrections and well-sourced challenges are welcome and are the fastest way the record improves. A public channel for submissions and disclosures will be listed here as the project opens to contributors.
