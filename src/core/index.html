Why it can be trusted

Authority is earned, not declared

This project makes no claim to final judgment. Its usefulness rests on three commitments: a published, versioned methodology anyone can critique; sourcing standards that keep unverifiable entries out; and a correction policy that treats being wrong in public as normal. Those rules live in the editorial protocol.
